The Window

Why now

  • £9,000 grant for oil and LPG heated homes runs from 21 July 2026 to 31 March 2027 — up from the standard £7,500.
  • 0% VAT on heat pump installations ends March 2027.
  • Oil and LPG are among the most expensive ways to heat a home. An air source heat pump typically cuts running costs and raises your EPC rating — which matters if you ever sell or remortgage.
